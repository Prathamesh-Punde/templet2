Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions

Purchase & Licensing

What is the pricing model for your software?

We offer a one-time purchase model with lifetime updates. No recurring subscription fees. Prices vary based on the product and number of users. Contact us for a detailed quote.

Do you offer free trials or demos?

Yes! We provide free demo sessions where our team will walk you through the features. You can also request a trial version for evaluation purposes.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfers, UPI, credit/debit cards, and cheques. For enterprise customers, we also offer installment payment options.

Installation & Setup

How long does installation take?

Standard installation takes 1-2 hours. Our team will handle the complete setup including database configuration and initial data import.

What are the system requirements?

Windows 7 or higher, 4GB RAM minimum (8GB recommended), 10GB free disk space, and an active internet connection for updates.

Do you provide training for staff?

Yes! We provide comprehensive training for your staff including hands-on sessions, video tutorials, and user manuals.

Data & Security

Is my data secure?

Absolutely! We use industry-standard encryption, secure databases, and regular backups. Your data is stored locally on your server for complete control.

Do you provide data backup solutions?

Yes, we offer both automatic local backups and cloud backup options. You can schedule daily, weekly, or monthly backups.

Can I migrate data from my existing system?

Yes! We assist with data migration from Excel, other billing software, or manual records. Our team ensures smooth transition with zero data loss.

Updates & Maintenance

Are updates free?

Yes! All software updates, bug fixes, and feature enhancements are included free for lifetime. We regularly release updates to improve functionality.

What if I need customization?

We offer customization services to tailor the software to your specific needs. Contact us with your requirements for a custom quote.

How often do you release updates?

We release minor updates monthly and major updates quarterly. Critical security patches are released immediately when needed.
